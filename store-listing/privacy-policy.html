Word Play — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Short version: Word Play itself collects no personal data at all. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The app contains ads powered by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (watch a short ad → earn game coins) and occasional full-screen ads between rounds (never during play). AdMob may collect standard advertising identifiers. Everything is playable offline, and offline no ads are shown.

1. Information We Collect

The developer of this app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no analytics, no trackers from us, and no accounts.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The games save your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, best scores and sound settings) in the app's local storage on your device only. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not uploaded to any server;
  • is removed automatically if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Play shows ads served by Google AdMob in two ways: optional rewarded video ads when you tap a reward button, and an occasional full-screen ad when you move on from a results screen (at most one every few rounds and a few minutes apart; never in the middle of a game). When an ad is loaded:

  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID) and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • This data is transmitted to Google over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form; you can change your choice any time under Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • You can opt out of interest-based advertising any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, or by resetting your advertising ID.
  • Google's privacy practices are described at policies.google.com.

All three games are fully playable offline.

4. Children's Privacy

Word Play is family-friendly and safe for children. The app contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no in-app purchases. Ad requests are tagged as child-directed, so Google AdMob serves only non-personalised, family-safe (G-rated) ads, and no personal data of children is collected by the developer. The advertising identifier is shared with ad networks only in the manner described in section 3.

5. Third Parties

Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above, the app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics services, and no ad networks other than Google AdMob.

6. Permissions

  • Internet — used only to load ads; all games work offline.
  • Vibrate — tiny haptic feedback during gameplay.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.
