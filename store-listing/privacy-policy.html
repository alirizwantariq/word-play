Word Play — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Short version: Word Play itself collects no personal data at all. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The app contains optional rewarded ads (watch a short ad → earn game coins) powered by Google AdMob, which may collect standard advertising identifiers. You can play everything offline without ever seeing an ad — ads only appear when you deliberately tap the "+100" reward button.

1. Information We Collect

The developer of this app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no analytics, no trackers from us, and no accounts.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The games save your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, best scores and sound settings) in the app's local storage on your device only. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not uploaded to any server;
  • is removed automatically if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Play includes optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob. If you tap the "+100" reward button:

  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID) and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • This data is transmitted to Google over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • You can opt out of interest-based advertising any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, or by resetting your advertising ID.
  • Google's privacy practices are described at policies.google.com.

All three games are fully playable offline; no ad is ever shown without you explicitly requesting one.

4. Children's Privacy

Word Play is family-friendly and safe for children. The app contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no in-app purchases. Children can play all games offline with no ads shown (rewarded ads require deliberately tapping the reward button, and no personal data of children is collected by the developer). The advertising identifier is shared with ad networks only in the manner described in section 3.

5. Third Parties

Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above, the app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics services, and no ad networks other than Google AdMob.

6. Permissions

  • Internet — used only to load optional rewarded ads; all games work offline.
  • Vibrate — tiny haptic feedback during gameplay.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.
